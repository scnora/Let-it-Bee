Our History

2019

In 2019, Bee Kind was founded by Sai Deja. The idea for the website came from her love of honey and bees, and her desire to share that passion with others. She started the website as a way to educate people about the importance of bees and the benefits of honey.

2022

In 2022, Bee Kind expanded its reach and started gaining a larger and loyal audience. This was the start of our growth and more traffic to our website. This year was also the first time we expanded to selling our honey at Farmers Markets in our local area. This is also when we started to expand our products to honey combs, soap, and candles.

2024

In 2024, Bee Kind continued to grow and started to gain recognition in the local honey market. We started to gain more traffic and even collaborated with other local busineses to sell our honey in their stores and let them use our honey in their products. This was a huge year for us as we also started to expand our online presence on social media. We also started to expand to educational content and products about bees and beekeeping for beginners.

2026

Right now in 2026, Bee Kind is a well established website with loyal audience and a strong presence in local honey market in New Jersey. We have a strong online presence and we are in the process of expanding our products to more local markets. We even have plans to lauch an online store in the near future, for our audience to be able to purchase our honey online. We are so grateful for all the support we have received from our audience and customers
